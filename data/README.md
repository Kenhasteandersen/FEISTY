The time series with environmental forcing data are obtained from GFDL-MOM6-COBALT for one grid cell (Liu et al., 2022). This grid cell is located in the Celtic Seas (mid-point longitude -12.5 degrees and latitude 52.5 degrees). For the same grid cell, fishing mortalities are estimated from the F/FMSY time series using van Denderen et al. (2024). To this end, we multiplied each F/FMSY value by 0.3, which approximates the FMSY in the environment. Fishing mortalities on mesopelagic and mid-water predators are set to 10% of the small and large pelagic fishing mortality, respectively. 

Source: 

van Denderen, P.D., Jacobsen, N., Andersen, K.H., Blanchard, J.L., Novaglio, C., Stock, C.A. and Petrik, C.M., 2024. Estimating fishing exploitation rates to simulate global catches and biomass changes of pelagic and demersal fish. Earth's Future, 12(10), p.e2024EF004604.
